sweep.wav - the chime played when a sweep starts.

This file was synthesised from scratch by tools/make_sweep_sound.py in the
source repository - no recordings, no third-party samples. Treat it as
public domain (CC0): use it, ship it, replace it.

To use your own sound, overwrite sweep.wav with any 16-bit PCM .wav
(44.1 kHz mono or stereo). Keep it short and quiet - the sweep fires
often. Volume and an on/off toggle live in the mod's menu under
Sense > Chime; the sound respects the game's own audio sliders.
